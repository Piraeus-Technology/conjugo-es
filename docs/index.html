Privacy Policy

ConjuGo ES — Spanish Verb Conjugations · Last updated: November 27, 2026

ConjuGo ES keeps your learning data on your device.

Overview

ConjuGo ES is an offline-first Spanish verb conjugation and practice app. Its dictionary, search, quizzes, flashcards, and learning progress work locally on your device. Pronunciation uses your device's speech service and may require an installed voice. Optional actions such as leaving a tip, rating the app, sharing it, sending email, or opening this policy use your device's store or system services and may require an internet connection.

Data Collection

The app does not ask for your name, email address, or other contact information. We do not collect location data, usage analytics, tracking data, or device identifiers. We do not use cookies or similar technologies.

Local Storage

ConjuGo ES stores the following information locally on your device: verbs you open, including when you open the Verb of the Day, and favorite verbs; quiz and flashcard progress, scores, streaks, and daily activity; spaced-repetition learning weights; selected practice tenses and CEFR levels; and preferences such as theme, automatic pronunciation, and whether to include vosotros.

ConjuGo ES does not transmit this learning data to us. To remove all local learning data and preferences, open the More tab and choose Reset Learning Data under Your Data.

Third-Party Services

ConjuGo ES does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

The optional Tip Jar uses Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Apple or Google processes the purchase under its own account and privacy terms. ConjuGo ES does not receive or store your payment-card details and a tip does not unlock an account or entitlement.

If you choose to rate or share the app, send feedback, or open an external link, your device opens the relevant app store, share sheet, email app, or web browser. Information you choose to provide through those services is handled by that service under its own privacy terms.

When you send us email, we receive your email address and the contents of your message so we can respond to your feedback.

Children's Privacy

The app does not ask anyone, including children under the age of 13, to provide personal information.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Given the nature of the app, we do not anticipate significant changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at contact@piraeus.app.
